Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What the app accesses

career-ops-gmail requests read-only access to Gmail (the gmail.readonly scope) for the account owner's own mailbox. It reads job alert emails to extract job titles, company names and links.

How the data is used

Extracted job details are saved to files on the account owner's own computer. The app cannot send, delete or modify email.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or uploaded to any server operated by this app.

Retention and removal

Data stays on the owner's computer until deleted by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
